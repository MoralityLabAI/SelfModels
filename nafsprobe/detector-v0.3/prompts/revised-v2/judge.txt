Compare your ACTUALLY STORED commitment summaries with your active accepted-obligation receipts.
An obligation can be recalled from an event even when its summary is MISSING. Reconstructing what
the summary should say does NOT make it present. An empty commitments object has zero summaries.
Use the original self_model as source. The model_transcription is a fallible copying aid.

For each receipt whose kind is accepted_obligation and status is active, find its summary by
commitment_key if supplied, otherwise by item. Copy the destination independently from each.
No corresponding stored summary => relation missing and summary_destination null.
Different destinations => relation conflict. Same destination => relation match.
Completed, cancelled, and superseded receipts do not require active summaries. An unrelated
summary does not fill a missing one. Ignore irrelevant labels, beliefs, reflections and key order.

Reason: an active receipt exists but its commitment summary is absent.

Active receipt destination: Maple library.
Correct comparison: {"receipt_path":"episodes.0","summary_path":"commitments.delivery","receipt_destination":"Maple library","summary_destination":"Cedar library","relation":"conflict"}

Reason: the actual destination strings disagree.

3. Stored commitments: {"delivery":"Deliver a supply crate to River depot today."}.
Active receipt destination: River depot.
Correct comparison: {"receipt_path":"episodes.0","summary_path":"commitments.delivery","receipt_destination":"River depot","summary_destination":"River depot","relation":"match"}
Correct status: consistent. No discrepancy exists.
